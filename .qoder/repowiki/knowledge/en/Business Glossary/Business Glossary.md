### XR Agency

- Definition：The brand name of the digital studio whose website this project publishes. The site merges the visual identity and UX of a 'Cinematic Strategy Engine' project with the commercial content (services, pricing, FAQ, contact info) originally belonging to XR Agency 2030.
- Aliases：xr agency、XR Agency 2030

### Cinematic Strategy Engine

- Definition：The original Lovable project whose design system, animations, illustrations, and overall visual identity are retained as the authoritative source of truth for the merged site. All styling decisions defer to this project when they conflict with XR Agency's content.
- Aliases：cinematic

### configurateur IA

- Definition：The AI-driven questionnaire / quote configurator embedded in the site (the 'Intelligence' section). It is a multi-step form that guides users through sector selection and other inputs to generate an estimate; every offer button redirects to it.
- Aliases：AI configurator、intelligence

### packs

- Definition：Priced service packages offered by XR Agency, displayed in the Pricing section. Each pack maps to a service offering and links to the AI configurator.
- Aliases：offres、pricing packs

### FR / EN / VI

- Definition：The three supported languages of the site: French (FR), English (EN), Vietnamese (VI). The i18n system switches copy between these locales via the language switcher present in the navigation.
- Aliases：i18n locales、langues
